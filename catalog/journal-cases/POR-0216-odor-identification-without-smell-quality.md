# Odors are detected by irritation but lack olfactory character

## Account

The patient had never experienced ordinary smell qualities. Strong substances could still be noticed through cooling, burning, or irritation, showing how trigeminal detection can masquerade as smelling without an olfactory percept.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

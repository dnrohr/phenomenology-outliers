# Fluent melody and prosody carry almost no propositional speech

## Account

A severely aphasic participant could produce familiar sung sequences more fluently than ordinary propositional utterances. Melody, rhythm, and overlearned words formed an output route where generative speech remained blocked.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

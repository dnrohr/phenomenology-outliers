# Objects appear but no coherent imagined setting forms

## Account

P02 could supply isolated objects and sensory details when asked to imagine a novel experience, yet they did not open into one contiguous place. The construction resembled separate images rather than a scene that could be surveyed from within.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# 58-year-old woman: known faces became unrecognizable clowns

## Account

Six hours before emergency assessment for a severe occipital headache, familiar faces in photographs became unrecognizable. She said faces looked like clowns and were especially distorted around the mouth. The face-specific anomaly was her only initial neurological deficit before imaging revealed intracranial hemorrhage. The report distinguishes intact detection of a face-like object from lost stable identity and altered local form.

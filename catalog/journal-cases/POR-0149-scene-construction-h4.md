# Imagined spatial continuation without normal recollection

## Account

H4 generated entities, sensory features, and spatial relations for unseen regions of photographs at control-like levels, then remembered fewer presented details. Scene completion and scene recollection diverged.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

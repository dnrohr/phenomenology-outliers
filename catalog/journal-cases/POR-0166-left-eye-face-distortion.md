# A left eye slides upward only in live and televised faces

## Account

The left eye of viewed faces seemed to move sideways and upward, but the distortion spared her own mirror image and photographs. Face recognition and nonface perception were normal. The symptom depended jointly on category and viewing condition.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Inner speech reports vanish while rhyme judgments partly survive

## Account

L.H. reported little accessible inner speech after aphasia, yet some tasks showed residual phonological manipulation without overt articulation. Conscious verbal rehearsal and implicit sound structure were not identical capacities.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Reasoning continues after the inner voice becomes unavailable

## Account

S.A. had profound grammatical language impairment but could solve nonverbal logic and numerical problems. Complex relations were available to thought without a fluent sentence-like medium.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

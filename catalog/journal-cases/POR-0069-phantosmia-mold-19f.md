# COVID phantosmia patient 6: intermittent mold smell

## Account

A 19-year-old woman developed a mold-like smell without an external source after COVID-19. She had reported neither anosmia nor hyposmia during the acute infection. The phantom appeared about twice weekly for less than a month, no sinonasal pathology was found, and it had ended by 48-day follow-up.

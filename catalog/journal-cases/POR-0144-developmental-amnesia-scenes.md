# A coherent imagined world despite developmental amnesia

## Account

Jon, whose hippocampal damage occurred early in life, produced imagined scenes with normal richness and spatial coherence despite severe episodic-memory impairment. Scene construction and remembering personal episodes were sharply dissociated.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# COVID phantosmia patient 4: persistent dirt smell

## Account

A 20-year-old woman perceived an unprompted dirt smell about three times weekly after COVID-19 with anosmia and hyposmia. The symptom began within the first month and persisted through 87-day follow-up. It caused nausea and difficulty eating severe enough that the clinicians prescribed carbamazepine. The case shows how an odor hallucination can directly reorganize appetite and food avoidance.

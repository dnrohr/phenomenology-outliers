# SDAM case AA: personal facts without episodic reliving

## Account

AA was one of three healthy adults reporting a lifelong inability to re-experience personal events despite intact knowledge of autobiographical facts and ordinary daily function. Laboratory testing supported severely reduced episodic detail rather than global amnesia. The case distinguishes knowing that an event happened from mentally traveling back into its scene and perspective.

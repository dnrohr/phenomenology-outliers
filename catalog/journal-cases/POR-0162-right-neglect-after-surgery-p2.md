# Surgery turns hemianopia into hemianopia plus neglect

## Account

P2 initially had right hemianopia without neglect. After tumor removal also interrupted splenial fibers, right visuospatial neglect appeared, exposing the difference between missing input and failure to represent or explore that side.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

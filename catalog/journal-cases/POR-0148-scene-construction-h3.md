# Beyond-the-frame space remains available in amnesia

## Account

H3 could extrapolate spatially beyond six presented scenes and usually preserve their central object, despite an overall memory deficit. The case preserves generative scene geometry alongside reduced episodic detail.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

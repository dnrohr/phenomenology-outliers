# Scene vividness survives impaired scene retention

## Account

H2 reported vivid, integrated imagined surroundings while performing like other hippocampal patients on poor later detail recall. The experienced quality of construction was not reducible to how much of the prompt could be retained.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

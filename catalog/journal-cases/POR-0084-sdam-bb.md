# SDAM case BB: intact life narrative without re-experiencing

## Account

BB likewise retained semantic knowledge about life history but reported almost no subjective reliving of specific episodes. The deficit was lifelong, not attributed to neurological injury or psychiatric illness, and was accompanied by weak visual imagery. Preserved intelligence and learning show that autobiographical facts can accumulate without the ordinary felt replay of lived moments.

# 79-year-old woman: the national anthem sung all day

## Account

She heard the Brazilian national anthem sung twenty-four hours a day while retaining insight. The report found no cognitive impairment, hearing loss, or prior psychiatric disorder and followed her for nine years; treatment reduced the symptom. The fixed, overlearned cultural content and ceaseless recurrence contrast intact musical structure with nearly absent control over onset and duration.

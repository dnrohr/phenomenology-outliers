# SKM: words instantly specify foods without a mouth-taste

## Account

For SKM, a word immediately and automatically specifies a precise food idea but does not create a literal taste in the mouth. “Dean,” for example, is minced beef in gravy. The report labels this an associator form. Comparing SKM with JIW demonstrates that equally stable lexical-food concurrents can differ phenomenally: conceptual certainty in one person, projected gustatory sensation in another.

# Pain is detected but not treated as threatening

## Account

Patient 1 identified pinpricks as painful but showed little withdrawal, distress, or defensive behavior. Nociceptive quality remained while its ordinary negative value was blunted.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

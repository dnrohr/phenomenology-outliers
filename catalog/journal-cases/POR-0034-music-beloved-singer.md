# 78-year-old woman: a beloved singer grew intolerably loud

## Account

She recognized vocals and accompaniment by a singer she had adored when young. Initially enjoyable, the music grew louder and became very disturbing. Bilateral sensorineural hearing loss was confirmed; there were no other psychotic or cognitive findings. Hearing aids ended the music, with no recurrence at one year. Familiar content changed from welcome to invasive through intensity alone.

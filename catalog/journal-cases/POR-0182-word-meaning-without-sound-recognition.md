# Audible words and sounds fail at an early temporal bottleneck

## Account

The patient detected sounds but needed an abnormally long silent gap to hear two brief tones as separate. Spoken words and environmental noises both lost meaning, while visual presentation of the named object could restore word identification. The deficit lay in early auditory organization rather than absent hearing.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Spinal-cord patient: two extra arms in stable postures

## Account

He reluctantly disclosed feeling two extra arms originating at the shoulders, equal in length to his real arms. They lay either crossed on his chest or extended over the abdomen with palms on thighs. Present daily for hours, they were strongest supine and in the morning and faded later in the day. The phantoms were nonpainful yet caused chest discomfort through their felt posture.

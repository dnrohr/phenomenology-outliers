# Dense amnesia with preserved coherent scene construction

## Account

P01 had dense amnesia and substantial bilateral hippocampal volume loss yet constructed rich, spatially coherent fictitious scenes near control levels. The outlier shows that impaired episodic memory need not entail an experienced inability to assemble new scenes.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

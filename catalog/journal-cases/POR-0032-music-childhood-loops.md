# 74-year-old woman: looped patriotic and childhood verses

## Account

She heard constant short verses of patriotic and children's songs remembered from childhood, looping repeatedly for four to six weeks. Conversation and television reduced them; solitude and driving heightened them. Worried she was “going crazy,” she nevertheless had normal mood, behavior, and neurological examination. Audiometry showed symmetric high-frequency hearing loss. Remote musical memory became an involuntary, context-sensitive auditory stream.

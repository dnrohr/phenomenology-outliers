# 85-year-old woman: a one-ear radio in three languages

## Account

She suddenly heard intermittent radio-like music only in her left ear: childhood tunes including Cantonese opera, a Putonghua folk song, and an English minstrel song, with voices and instruments. The episodes persisted three months. Severe left and moderate right hearing loss plus an auditory-radiation infarct were documented. Remote memory, multiple languages, musical form, and lateralized external location were bound together.

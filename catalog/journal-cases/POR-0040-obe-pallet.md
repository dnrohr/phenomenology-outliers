# Participant 2: rising from the body while operating a pallet truck

## Account

While operating a heavy pallet truck he felt something inside rising and trying to leave. He sped up to stay attached to body and matter. He stopped feeling his body's weight and the truck's heaviness, feared ceasing to exist materially, and regarded the event as real. A first meditation session preceded it. Motor action became an intentional anchor against loss of embodiment.

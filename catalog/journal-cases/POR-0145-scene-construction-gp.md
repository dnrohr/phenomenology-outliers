# Vivid scene extension with almost no memory for the viewed scene

## Account

G.P. readily imagined what lay beyond photographs, rated the results vivid and coherent, and produced spatial details. Moments later he could not identify the central object from the viewed scene, separating online scene extension from retention of its contents.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# An unseen companion arrives with an unowned hand

## Account

Patient 1 simultaneously sensed another person nearby and denied ownership of a hand. An extracorporeal social presence and a local failure of body ownership emerged together after right superior parietal stroke.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

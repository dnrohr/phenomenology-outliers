# Imagined elements have nothing spatial to hang on

## Account

P03 generated relevant components of proposed events but reported difficulty arranging them into stable surrounding space. Detail generation survived better than the framework that binds details into an experienced environment.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

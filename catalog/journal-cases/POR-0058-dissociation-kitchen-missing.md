# P16: returning to a world made too large after the kitchen disappeared

## Account

When P16 emerged from dissociation, everything could seem unusually large, with the strange implication that the kitchen had been missing during the state. At other times colors became brighter or space seemed more expansive. The report records derealization not merely as abstract unreality but as changes in scale, color intensity, spatial extent, and continuity of the surrounding scene.

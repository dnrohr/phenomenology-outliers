# P2: urge to take the body apart and sew it together again

## Account

P2 described eyes, head, and arms as so strangely configured that they felt an urge to take everything out and sew it back together. They sometimes watched themselves from behind like a film; the world could feel like a glitching, incomplete game. Trauma memories made surroundings shut off and could coincide with felt hands holding the shoulders or mouth and distorting the face. Voices sometimes precipitated emotional shutdown.

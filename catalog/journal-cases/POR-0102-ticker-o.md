# O: vivid inner print supported backward speech and sentence spelling

## Account

The paper recounts O, a young woman with vivid, uncolored visualization of words. She was proficient at speaking backward and could spell whole sentences backward, suggesting that the internally seen orthographic sequence remained available for unusual manipulation. The source is a secondary discussion of the original case, so this record confines itself to the reported visual text and associated abilities.

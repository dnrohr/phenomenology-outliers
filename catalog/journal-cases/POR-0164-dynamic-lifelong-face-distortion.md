# Faces move, shrink, rotate, and exchange identities

## Account

Zed has always seen facial features move, vanish, droop, rotate, expand, and shrink. He can still judge age and sex finely, but familiar faces may look like strangers and strangers like known people. Shape distortion and several forms of face knowledge dissociate.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

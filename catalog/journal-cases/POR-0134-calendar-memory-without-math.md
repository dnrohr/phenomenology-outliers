# An immense calendar is retrieved as knowing, not calculation

## Account

A.C.596 answered date questions within seconds and could flexibly retrieve day, month, or year, while formal mathematics was poor. He denied using an algorithm and repeatedly characterized the answer as simply known. Accuracy was best within his lifetime, favoring a vast semantic calendar over explicit computation.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Music is recognized perfectly but gives no pleasure

## Account

The participant perceived melodies and understood which music others would find moving, yet experienced little pleasure and showed weak autonomic reward responses to music. Monetary reward remained effective, isolating pleasure by domain.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

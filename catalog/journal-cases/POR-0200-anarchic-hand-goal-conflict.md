# One hand performs a goal the person rejects

## Account

The affected hand initiated organized, object-directed actions that conflicted with the patient's stated intention, while ownership of the limb remained. Goal selection and limb ownership came apart.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

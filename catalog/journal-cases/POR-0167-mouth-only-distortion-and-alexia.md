# Every mouth enlarges while the rest of the face stays stable

## Account

The patient's perceived distortion was confined to other people's mouths, which looked enlarged and misshapen; eyes, noses, landscapes, and other objects remained stable. He also had difficulty reading letters and numbers despite preserved writing.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

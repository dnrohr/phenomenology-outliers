# A whole left side drops from picture description

## Account

During an acute right-sided stroke, the woman described only the right half of a complex picture and spontaneously held gaze rightward despite full eye movements. Neglect improved rapidly, while unawareness of the stroke and residual deficits persisted.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

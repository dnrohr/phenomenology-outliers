# A familiar language sounds foreign while reading stays normal

## Account

The patient understood lexical content yet had difficulty extracting speaker identity, affective tone, and melodic contour. Words remained meaningful while the patterned vocal information carried alongside them became inaccessible.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# 38-year-old man: recorded pistol-shot sensation during sleep

## Account

For four years he experienced a pistol-shot sound one or two times weekly amid nightmares, sleepwalking, sleep talking, bruxism, and sleep-related eating. He also had irresistible sleep attacks and sleep-transition hallucinations. One pistol-shot episode was captured as he aroused from N2 sleep. Unlike an actual gunshot or headache, the perceived blast left no pain or external evidence.

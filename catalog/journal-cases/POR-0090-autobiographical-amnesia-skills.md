# Middle-aged psychiatrist: personal identity erased while medical skills remained

## Account

After a motor-vehicle accident, a middle-aged man lost nearly all autobiographical memory and personal identity, both retrograde and anterograde. Yet a broad repertoire of medical knowledge and procedural skills remained, leading him to infer that he had worked as a psychiatrist. The case sharply separates knowing how to perform a professional role from remembering the life in which that role was learned.

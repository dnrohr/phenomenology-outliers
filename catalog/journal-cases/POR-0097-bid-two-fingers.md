# 20-year-old man: owned fingers that should not belong to his body

## Account

Since childhood he recognized the fourth and fifth fingers of his left hand as physically his yet felt they should not belong to his body. The persistent mismatch produced profound distress and nightmares despite psychotherapy and medication. After carefully evaluated elective amputation, he reported immediate relief, ended nightmares, and improved function. Ownership knowledge and desired body identity had pointed in opposite directions.

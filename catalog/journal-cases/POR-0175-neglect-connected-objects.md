# Connecting two shapes makes the left one vanish more often

## Account

C1 omitted more left-side targets when left and right items were visibly connected into one object. Grouping did not rescue the neglected component; it intensified competition within the represented whole.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Woman whose familiar voice abruptly sounded Irish after tonsillectomy

## Account

After tonsil surgery, family, friends, and media listeners heard her pitch, tone, and timing as an Irish accent despite no intention to imitate one. The change made her own produced voice socially unfamiliar even though propositional language remained hers. The case is cataloged for altered prosodic identity and the mismatch between intended ordinary speech and how articulation was heard by self and others.

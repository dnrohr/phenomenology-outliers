# Recent experiences remain vivid while word and world knowledge erode

## Account

With semantic dementia, L.S.J. could recount relatively recent personal experiences in detail while names, concepts, and older knowledge progressively degraded. A lived episode could remain accessible after parts of the conceptual system needed to describe the world were lost.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

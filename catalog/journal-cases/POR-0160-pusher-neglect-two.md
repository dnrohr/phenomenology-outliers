# The body is driven toward the neglected side

## Account

Patient 2 pushed with the unaffected limbs, shifted his center leftward, and resisted rightward correction despite being unable to stand independently. Perceived upright, action, and external vertical were misaligned.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

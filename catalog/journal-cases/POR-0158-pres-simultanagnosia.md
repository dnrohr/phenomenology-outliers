# Perfect acuity without a usable visual world

## Account

After eclampsia and posterior reversible encephalopathy, the patient regained 20/20 acuity, normal fields, and normal contrast sensitivity but could not efficiently perceive multiple elements together. Strategy training improved daily function even though standard eye measures had already normalized.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

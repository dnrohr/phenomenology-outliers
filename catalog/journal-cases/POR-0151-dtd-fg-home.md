# Getting lost even inside a new home

## Account

F.G. never developed reliable route learning, landmark use, map reading, or spatial-part integration. After moving, he could become lost inside his own home, especially at night. Unlike Pt1, he had no effective verbal or procedural workaround.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

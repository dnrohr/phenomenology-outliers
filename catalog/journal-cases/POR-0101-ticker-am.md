# AM: spoken sentences scroll as imaginal subtitles

## Account

AM, a woman in her early twenties, vividly experienced uncolored written words whenever she heard, spoke, or verbally thought. Longer words and sentences moved from right to left through imaginal space like subtitles. She could enumerate letters in long words and words in sentences with exceptional speed. The report distinguishes automatic orthographic imagery from ordinary deliberate spelling.

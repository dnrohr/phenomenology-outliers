# Recognized landmarks lack directional value

## Account

After cerebral hypoxia, G.R. recognized landmarks yet could not reliably retain which direction each implied. Premorbid knowledge of his town and famous buildings remained, while new maze routes were nearly impossible unless distinctive visual cues were added.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

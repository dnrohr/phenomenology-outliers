# Patient M: speech recovered while everyday sounds lost meaning

## Account

Patient M chronically failed to recognize nonverbal environmental sounds while speech comprehension was spared. The authors characterize this as associative auditory agnosia: acoustic events were heard but did not reliably reach object meaning. His unusual recovery profile reverses pure word deafness, demonstrating separable routes from sound to linguistic and environmental significance.

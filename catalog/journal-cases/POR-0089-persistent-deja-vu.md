# 23-year-old man: persistent debilitating déjà vu

## Account

For about three years a 23-year-old man lived with persistent, debilitating déjà vu alongside anxiety and depersonalization. New situations repeatedly arrived with inappropriate familiarity rather than the brief isolated flash common in ordinary déjà vu. EEG and MRI found no neurological abnormality. The report documents how a normally momentary metacognitive conflict can become a continuing background condition.

# Participant 4: rising above the bedroom and then the sea

## Account

Lying supine, he separated, saw himself sleeping from above, rose beyond the room, and then occupied a viewpoint over the sea. The state was real, calm, comfortable, and pleasant. Return brought a falling sensation and abrupt awakening while sweating. The report traces a continuous expansion of represented space followed by a sharp vestibular-style descent.

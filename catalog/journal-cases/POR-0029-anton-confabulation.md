# 83-year-old woman: blindness denied and surroundings confabulated

## Account

After bilateral occipital infarcts she was cortically blind but denied visual loss. She walked into objects while acting as if she could see and supplied descriptions of surroundings unsupported by available vision. The source calls these descriptions confabulations. Her case separates absent visual capacity from intact confidence in seeing and from continued generation of scene claims.

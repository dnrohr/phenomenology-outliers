# Upright feels wrong and correction is resisted

## Account

Patient 1 leaned severely left while sitting or standing and actively resisted being moved toward objective upright. Left neglect and reduced left-body sensation coexisted with a distorted postural reference frame.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# P19: hands do things without feeling like their hands

## Account

While using their hands, P19 sometimes felt that the hands were not theirs and that they were not doing the action. They also felt numb where other people would experience extreme emotions. Looking from outside could produce the judgment “that's not me.” The detachment prompted fear of abnormality and of not feeling anything, combining altered ownership, reduced agency, and affective blunting.

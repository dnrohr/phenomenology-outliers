# Arithmetic survives when familiar numbers lose their meaning

## Account

K.B. could compare ordinary numerals and calculate expressions such as six times nine, yet familiar dates and numerical labels no longer reliably evoked what they conventionally denoted. The value twenty-five remained available as a quantity while its link to Christmas Day could fail.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Calendar answers extend from 1591 to 8378

## Account

G.W. rapidly named weekdays for dates across millennia, drew at a professional level, and remembered large sets of city populations. He could not fully verbalize the calendar method, but response patterns showed reuse of calendar templates, combining rule knowledge with exceptional numerical memory.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Multiplication facts fail more than addition and subtraction

## Account

Following a left subcortical lesion, B.O.O. was disproportionately impaired on simple multiplication while addition and subtraction were less affected. The profile isolates learned verbal arithmetic facts from quantity-based operations that can be reconstructed.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# 86-year-old woman: continuous familiar pop music she could retune

## Account

As hearing worsened she heard continuous bilateral music at normal volume: often Frank Sinatra, seasonally Christmas songs, and sometimes unpleasant unfamiliar music like a broken record. She first blamed neighbors, then realized others heard nothing. Singing along could change the tune. Television remained usable and distress was limited, producing a rare combination of persistent involuntary sound and partial control over content.

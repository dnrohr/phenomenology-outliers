# Touch is felt but cannot be placed on the limb

## Account

Patient 3 reported an unseen presence and could detect touch below the elbow or knee without identifying where it occurred. Sensation survived while its placement on the body model failed.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

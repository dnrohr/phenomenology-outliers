# 86-year-old woman: vivid household scenes and faces

## Account

For one week, mainly in the evening, she saw vivid moving household objects, people, and faces on ceilings or in corners. Episodes were binocular and lasted hours, without auditory or other sensory counterparts. She remained alert, scored 30/30 cognitively, and knew the scenes were unreal, yet feared she was “going crazy.” She had glaucoma, macular degeneration, and cataracts. The images later resolved.

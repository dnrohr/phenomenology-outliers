# Unrecognizable music still gives pleasure

## Account

The patient detected sounds but increasingly could not identify speech, environmental noises, or musical pieces. He nevertheless reported genuine pleasure from favored jazz and classical music, separating recognition of auditory objects from affective response to them.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

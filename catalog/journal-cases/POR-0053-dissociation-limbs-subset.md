# P23: limbs with minds of their own

## Account

P23 felt that most limbs had “a mind of their own,” yet described them as a subset of self rather than wholly separate agents. This is a graded disturbance: the limbs remained included within a broader self-model while local action no longer felt fully authored. The source places the report among changes in bodily awareness, ownership, and control.

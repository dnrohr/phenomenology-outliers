# Participant 1: calm unfolding after orgasm

## Account

After sex and orgasm while taking antidepressants, she remained physically in a body yet saw everything from above and called it an unfolding. She felt calm, relaxed, strange, and astonished rather than afraid; it seemed fully real. She interpreted the displaced center as consciousness rather than a soul, would repeat it, and reported no change in worldview or fear of death.

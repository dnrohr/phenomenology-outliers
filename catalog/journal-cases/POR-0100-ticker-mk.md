# MK: every spoken word appears as black print in the mind

## Account

Whenever MK heard speech—including his own voice or unattended travelers—or spoke inwardly, each word immediately appeared in written form. Letters were distinct, black, normally sized, upper- or lowercase, about one word at a time, located in his head rather than fixed external space. He could not suppress it. Wordless music produced no text. He discovered only at 68 that this lifelong experience was unusual.

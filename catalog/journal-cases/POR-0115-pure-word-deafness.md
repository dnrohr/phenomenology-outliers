# 19-year-old woman: phones and animals audible, speech incomprehensible

## Account

After acute hearing deterioration, she remained unable to follow spoken commands yet turned toward a ringing phone and other sudden sounds. She identified laughter, music, babies, instruments, phones, and animal cries, while auditory comprehension and repetition were severely impaired. Reading, spontaneous speech, and writing remained good. Sound detection and environmental recognition survived while speech ceased to resolve into words.

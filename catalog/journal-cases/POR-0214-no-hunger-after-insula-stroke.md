# The body needs food without producing hunger

## Account

After a left insular-region stroke, the patient reported that he no longer became hungry and often ate only from habit. Formerly favored textures became aversive, portions shrank, and weight fell, separating habitual eating from appetite and food reward.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

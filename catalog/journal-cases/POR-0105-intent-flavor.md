# Reddit self-report: conversational intention, not words alone, has flavor

## Account

This poster reported no taste in ordinary conversation unless words carried a particular intention. Lies were bitter and nauseating; white lies tasted of too much butter; flirting made mild sweetness at the tongue tip; cheesy pickup lines were sickeningly maple-syrup sweet. Some moderate sounds translated into pain, while hearing licking or spitting could produce a felt trace of another person's tongue or saliva on the body. The self-report explicitly questions rather than fixes a diagnosis.

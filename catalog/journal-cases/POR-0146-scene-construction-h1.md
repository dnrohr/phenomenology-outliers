# Spatially coherent imagination with sparse immediate recall

## Account

H1 imagined plausible continuations beyond a photograph's borders and endorsed a coherent inner layout, yet recalled markedly fewer elements of the photograph afterward. Construction succeeded where recent scene memory failed.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Melodies change without sounding wrong

## Account

Monica had lifelong difficulty recognizing tunes or noticing an out-of-key note despite normal hearing, intelligence, and memory outside music. Large pitch movements were accessible, while the fine-grained contour needed for ordinary melody experience was not.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

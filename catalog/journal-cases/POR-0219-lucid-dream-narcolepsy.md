# Dream insight becomes frequent and controllable

## Account

The participant frequently recognized dreams as dreams and could sometimes alter their course, especially during fragmented REM sleep. Metacognitive insight that is usually weak during dreaming became unusually available.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

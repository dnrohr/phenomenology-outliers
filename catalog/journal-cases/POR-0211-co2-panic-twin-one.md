# A person described as fearless panics at rising carbon dioxide

## Account

Twin 1 had bilateral amygdala damage and little everyday fear, yet carbon-dioxide inhalation produced panic and urgent air hunger. Conscious fear could be generated by bodily chemistry without the usual external-threat pathway.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

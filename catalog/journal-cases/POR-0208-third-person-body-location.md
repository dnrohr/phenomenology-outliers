# Self-location shifts to a point outside the body

## Account

The patient experienced a second body and an unstable viewpoint that could alternate between the physical body and an external position. Visual perspective, self-location, and body ownership no longer converged on one place.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

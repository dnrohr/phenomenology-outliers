# Verbal directions succeed where maps and mental images fail

## Account

C.A. could not learn new routes from experience, a simple plan, a map, or an image of the school environment. Verbally sequenced directions remained usable, and explicit landmark-and-route training improved school navigation.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

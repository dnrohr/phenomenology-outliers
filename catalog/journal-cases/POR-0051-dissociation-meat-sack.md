# P6: piloting a body that does not feel owned

## Account

P6 said the body did not feel like theirs and compared embodiment to “piloting a meat sack.” The metaphor preserves executive control while removing ordinary mineness: there is a controller and an operated biological object rather than a transparent lived body. The study reports this under altered awareness, ownership, and control, without claiming that the participant literally believed the body belonged to someone else.

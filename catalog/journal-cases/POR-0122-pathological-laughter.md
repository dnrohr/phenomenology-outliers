# 76-year-old woman: laughter without amusement and full embarrassment

## Account

After recovering consciousness following hemorrhage and infarction, she developed involuntary bouts of laughter unrelated to situation or inner emotion. She remained fully conscious, could not control the expression, and felt embarrassed. There was no matching inappropriate crying. Fear of laughing at the wrong moment led her to withdraw socially. The case dissociates facial-vocal emotional display from felt amusement and voluntary action.

# JIW: each word arrives as a droplet of flavor

## Account

JIW experiences words as veridical flavor sensations in the mouth, each like a droplet landing on the tongue. Complex components can include texture and temperature: “jail,” for example, evokes cold hard bacon. The paper calls this a projector form, contrasting actual-seeming oral sensation with purely conceptual food association. Persistent tastes can make reading and conversation unpleasant.

# 38-year-old woman: husband known only by voice and body

## Account

For five days after an occipital infarct, she could not recognize her husband by face, identifying him only from voice and body shape. At a funeral she used relatives' hair patterns. Face failure coexisted with visual-field loss and later left slower reading and weaker visual memory. The episode demonstrates rapid substitution of voice, hair, and body cues for absent facial identity.

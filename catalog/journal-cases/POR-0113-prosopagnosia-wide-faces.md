# 69-year-old woman: familiar faces widened until everyone looked alike

## Account

Watching television, she saw older people as if after facelifts and all faces wider at the jaw. News presenters and a political candidate looked wrong. Within days she could not recognize even close acquaintances; family photographs all looked alike and a friend became identifiable only on speaking. Body weight also appeared distorted and facial attractiveness differences vanished, while reading remained intact.

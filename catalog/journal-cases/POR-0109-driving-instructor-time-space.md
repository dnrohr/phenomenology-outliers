# Driving instructor: preserved routes but disrupted temporal and topographic orientation

## Account

After a brain hemorrhage, a professional driving instructor developed anterograde episodic amnesia and disturbances in mental time and orientation, despite previously expert route knowledge. The study includes his videotaped comments and tests autobiographical memory, temporal placement, and navigation. His case shows that being skilled at guiding others through public space does not protect the internal map of when and where from selective disruption.

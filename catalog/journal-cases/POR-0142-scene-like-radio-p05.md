# Events can be described without a visual scene opening up

## Account

P05 could think of things happening and describe them, but the result felt more like receiving a radio account than watching a scene. Propositional event content remained while scene-like presence and spatial integration were weak.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Participant 10: travel to a vividly remembered Scottish village

## Account

She decided to travel to Scotland, felt absorbed there, viewed landscapes and a village, and retained place names. Later she searched and believed photographs matched the cabins and places seen. Reporting many episodes since age seven, she described immense peace, expanded consciousness, ordinary life as less real, and post-travel exhaustion. The claimed match is preserved at face value.

# Man with epilepsy: ordinary familiarity versus fearful predictive déjà vu

## Account

A man familiar with both ordinary and seizure-related déjà vu said the epileptic form carried worry, fear, and dread. It felt so familiar that he seemed able to see the next step and predict what would happen. Common déjà vu lacked that predictive force. He distinguished familiarity as a feeling from déjà vu as a sensation, demonstrating metacognitive access to two superficially similar recognition anomalies.

# BIID patient 1: simulating a right below-knee amputation to feel complete

## Account

Since seeing a child with an amputation, he had felt a persistent need for right below-knee amputation to become complete. He sat in a wheelchair and folded and tied the leg to resemble a stump, but numbness limited simulation to about an hour. He considered self-amputation while recognizing its danger. The unwanted limb remained functional yet alien to his preferred bodily identity.

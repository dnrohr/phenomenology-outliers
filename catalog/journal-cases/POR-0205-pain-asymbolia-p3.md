# A painful stimulus is acknowledged almost neutrally

## Account

Patient 3 verbally recognized painful stimulation while remaining unusually indifferent and failing to protect the stimulated body part. Knowing the stimulus category did not restore motivational force.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

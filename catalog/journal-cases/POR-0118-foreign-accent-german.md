# Italian man: permanent German-sounding intonation

## Account

After cyclosporine encephalopathy, a native Italian speaker developed speech consistently heard as carrying German intonation. Years later cognition and neurological examination were otherwise normal, but the accent remained. He did not acquire German language knowledge; rather, timing and prosodic changes made native speech sound foreign, separating linguistic content from vocal identity.

# Touch, warmth, and pressure remain while injury does not hurt

## Account

The participant could feel ordinary contact and temperature changes but did not experience tissue-damaging events as pain. Somatic awareness was not globally absent; the protective nociceptive channel was selectively missing.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

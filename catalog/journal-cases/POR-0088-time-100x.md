# Young man: time felt one hundred times slower

## Account

During evolving encephalitis, his sense of time slowing intensified alongside anxiety and near-total insomnia. One day before admission it felt tenfold slower; on admission he estimated up to one hundredfold slowing. The magnitude was a subjective comparison, not clock measurement, but conveys an extreme divergence between experienced passage and public time.

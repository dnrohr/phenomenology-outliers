# SDAM case CC: lifelong absence of mental time travel

## Account

CC completed the original three-person SDAM series. Personal history remained available as information, while specific past scenes could not be recollected with a first-person sense of re-experiencing. The researchers also examined future simulation, because constructing a possible episode and reconstructing a past one share mental-time-travel demands. The case is cataloged separately as an individual participant, not a population average.

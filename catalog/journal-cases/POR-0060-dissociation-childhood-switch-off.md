# P15: switching off into an unfamiliar world

## Account

P15 connected an ability to “switch off” with coping during childhood trauma. In that mode surroundings felt strange, which increased anxiety and mistrust of other people. The persistence of the episodes led to frustration and worry that they might never stop. The report traces a chain from emotional trigger to environmental unfamiliarity to altered social interpretation.

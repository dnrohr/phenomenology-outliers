# Vivid objects appear without a stable surrounding layout

## Account

The complementary patient reported vivid object form and color but difficulty maintaining relative positions or mentally rotating a layout. Image vividness did not guarantee a navigable scene representation.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

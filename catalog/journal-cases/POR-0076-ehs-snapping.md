# 13-year-old boy: a single snapping sound just after sleep onset

## Account

A 13-year-old boy heard a snapping sound inside his head immediately after falling asleep, two or three times per month and never more than once nightly. Position made no difference. He also talked and walked in sleep and used electronic devices heavily near bedtime. Sleep-behavior education improved all symptoms; the snapping events remained absent for two years.

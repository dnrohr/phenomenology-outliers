# 63-year-old man: instant total taste loss and tongue numbness during lunch

## Account

During lunch, a 63-year-old man felt a brief frontal headache followed instantly by total taste loss and numbness of the whole tongue. Two months later formal testing confirmed bilateral ageusia while smell remained normal. He rated taste 2/100 and life-quality impairment 97/100, reporting lost appetite and no enjoyment of eating or cooking. The case separates gustatory absence from preserved olfaction and shows its strong motivational cost.

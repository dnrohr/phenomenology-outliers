# 46-year-old woman: an internal bang and fear of returning to sleep

## Account

For two years she woke at sleep onset to a loud bang apparently inside her head, more than weekly and without pain. Distress produced persistent fear of returning to sleep. Supine posture increased episodes, so she learned to avoid it. Sleep recording was unremarkable; education and amitriptyline eventually brought remission. The sound had abrupt perceptual force but no external source or nociceptive component.

# A right visual field is present but behaviorally omitted

## Account

After a left posterior stroke damaged both primary visual pathways and splenial connections, P1 showed chronic right-sided neglect in addition to right hemianopia. The inaccessible side affected exploratory representation, not merely retinal visibility.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

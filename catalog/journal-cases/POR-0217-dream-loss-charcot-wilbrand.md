# Visual dreams vanish while waking vision recovers

## Account

After posterior brain injury, the patient reported that dreams no longer contained visual scenes and voluntary visual imagery was severely reduced, despite useful waking sight. Perception and internally generated nocturnal vision separated.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

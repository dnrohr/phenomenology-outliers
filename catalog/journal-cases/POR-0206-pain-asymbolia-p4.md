# Pain knowledge does not trigger avoidance

## Account

Patient 4 could report pain yet tolerated repeated stimulation without normal escape or protest. Report, affect, and action were separately visible components.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

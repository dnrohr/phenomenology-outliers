# Sour disappears while sweet, bitter, and salty tastes remain

## Account

The participant was unresponsive to sour stimuli on the anterior tongue while responding normally to bitter, sweet, and salty tastes. Taste was therefore not globally weak: one qualitative channel was selectively absent within an otherwise differentiated gustatory world.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# COVID phantosmia patient 2: recurring phantom soap odor

## Account

A 26-year-old man who had experienced both smell loss and reduction during COVID-19 later perceived a soap odor with no source. It recurred roughly three times weekly for one to three months. Examination found no sinonasal pathology, and the symptom had resolved by the 32-day follow-up recorded in the case table.

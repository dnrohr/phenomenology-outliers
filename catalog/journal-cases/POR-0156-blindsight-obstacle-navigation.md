# Unseen obstacles guide a successful walk

## Account

Cortically blind T.N. reported no visual awareness yet spontaneously walked around boxes and other obstacles without collision. Visually guided locomotion remained possible without the ordinary scene appearing in consciousness.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

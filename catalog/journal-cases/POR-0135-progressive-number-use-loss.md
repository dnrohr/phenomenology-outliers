# Checks, change, and written calculation progressively fail

## Account

A woman with corticobasal degeneration developed disproportionate difficulty handling money, balancing a checkbook, reading multidigit numbers, and carrying out written arithmetic. Memory, ordinary reading, and speech were initially much better preserved, making numerical notation and calculation a leading edge of decline.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

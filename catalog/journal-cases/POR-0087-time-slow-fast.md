# 38-year-old woman: time alternately crawled and raced

## Account

She repeatedly felt time moving too slowly—the traffic light seemed never to turn green and waiting became intolerable—and, less often, too fast, as though time had slipped. In another symptom, a television scene seemed destined to proceed a particular way; deviation from the spontaneously predicted sequence caused anxiety. The source documents independent and combined episodes rather than a stable belief about clocks.

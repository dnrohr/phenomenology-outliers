# Object unity deepens spatial omission

## Account

C2 detected isolated left items better than equally placed items joined to right-side partners. What counted as one object changed access to one side of it.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

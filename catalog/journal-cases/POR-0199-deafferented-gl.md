# Movement guided by vision without felt limb position

## Account

After large-fiber sensory loss, G.L. could move deliberately when watching her limbs but had little ordinary sense of where they were. In darkness, posture and trajectory became profoundly unstable. Vision substituted for missing bodily position experience.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

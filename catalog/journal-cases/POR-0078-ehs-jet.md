# 77-year-old man: a jet flying inside his head for thirty years

## Account

For over thirty years he awoke at sleep onset to the sound of a flying jet inside his head. Events occurred three or four times monthly, particularly supine, caused distress, and never caused pain. They had long been attributed to stress. Sleep hygiene alone did not help; low-dose amitriptyline reduced frequency and then produced remission.

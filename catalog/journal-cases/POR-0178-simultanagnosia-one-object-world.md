# A crowded world resolves into one object at a time

## Account

The patient could identify a locally attended object but not consciously apprehend several objects as a unified display. The bottleneck affected scene multiplicity rather than basic acuity or single-object recognition.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

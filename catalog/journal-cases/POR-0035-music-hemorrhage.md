# 29-year-old woman: music after cerebral hemorrhage

## Account

A 29-year-old woman reported a week of hearing music while hospitalized after sudden severe headache and intraventricular and intracerebral hemorrhages. The series uses her acute neurological onset to contrast hearing-loss and psychiatric cases. Because it gives limited first-person texture, this record does not invent a tune, location, loudness, or emotional response; its key feature is the close onset relation to an acute cerebral event.

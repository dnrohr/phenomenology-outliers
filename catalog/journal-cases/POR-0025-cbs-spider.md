# 69-year-old man: spider, faces, animals, and familiar visitors

## Account

For six months, mostly evenings, he saw faces, odd shapes, animals, a spider, and familiar people sitting in his armchair. He once tried to kill the spider, showing how immediately convincing an image could be, while otherwise retaining insight and clear cognition. The experiences followed abrupt visual distortion and occurred with exudative macular degeneration. The case contrasts perceptual presence, automatic action, and later reflective correction.

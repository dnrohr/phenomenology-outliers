# Future questions yield facts instead of experienced scenes

## Account

D.B. could state generic facts about what might happen but struggled to construct a specific, spatially situated future event. Possibility knowledge persisted without pre-experiencing.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Time and place briefly lose their coordinates

## Account

During focal episodes, patient 1 remained awake yet could not anchor the current date and location. Orientation returned between events, making the loss sharply state-dependent rather than a permanent knowledge deficit.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

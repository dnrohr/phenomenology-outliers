# Numbers become the first unusable part of daily life

## Account

This patient presented with progressive inability to write checks, manage a checkbook, and perform calculations, rather than a memory-first complaint. Numerical symbols, quantities, and procedures became functionally inaccessible before the more familiar global picture of Alzheimer disease emerged.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

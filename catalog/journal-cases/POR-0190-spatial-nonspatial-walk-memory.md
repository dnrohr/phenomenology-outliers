# Spatial and nonspatial details fade together after a real walk

## Account

This patient remembered fewer elements of an observed campus walk, but spatial and nonspatial contents were reduced to a similar degree. The profile argues against a purely spatial memory loss while highlighting severe disruption of chronological order.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

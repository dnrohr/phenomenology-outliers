# The reaching hand is magnetically drawn toward where the eyes look

## Account

J.J. could see targets yet his reaching errors depended jointly on which hand moved and where his eyes were directed. Eye and limb coordinates became difficult to decouple, even though target presence was consciously available.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

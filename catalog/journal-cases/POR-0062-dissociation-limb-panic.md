# P18: inability to command limbs triggers panic

## Account

P18 reported episodes in which the limbs would not do what was intended. The altered agency itself provoked panic. The source offers little further participant-specific detail, so this record keeps the boundary tight: it documents subjectively impaired command and the immediate affective consequence, not paralysis, anatomical damage, or a belief that another agent controlled the limbs.

# COVID phantosmia patient 8: frequent persistent food odor

## Account

After acute anosmia and hyposmia, a 36-year-old man perceived phantom food odors about five times per week—the highest frequency in this nine-person series. The symptom was initially under a month in duration but persisted to 96-day follow-up. No sinonasal pathology was identified.

# 62-year-old man: externally located nursery rhymes in one ear

## Account

For six months he heard familiar songs and nursery rhymes several times daily, especially in quiet. They were located externally on the left, uncontrollable, intrusive, and disruptive. Onset was abrupt without other neurological symptoms, against a twenty-year history of mild hearing loss. The report distinguishes music imagined “in the head” from a percept assigned to one side of external auditory space.

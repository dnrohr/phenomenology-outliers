# COVID phantosmia patient 9: transient dirt smell

## Account

An 18-year-old woman developed a dirt-like phantom odor around three times weekly after COVID-19. She had reported no acute smell loss or reduction, and examination found no sinonasal disease. The experience lasted less than one month and had resolved by 54-day follow-up.

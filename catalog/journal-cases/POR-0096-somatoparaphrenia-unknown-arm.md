# Patient P2: a left arm certainly not his, but belonging to no one known

## Account

After surgery for a right-sided brain lesion, P2 firmly and continuously denied that his left arm belonged to him, though he could not identify an alternative owner. Ownership of the left leg and right limbs remained. He was aware of illness and motor impairment but unaware of sensory and visual-field loss, showing that insight varied across deficits within one person.

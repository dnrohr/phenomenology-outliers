# COVID phantosmia patient 7: persistent dirt smell

## Account

A 35-year-old man developed a dirt odor with no stimulus about four times each week after COVID-19, despite no reported acute anosmia or hyposmia. The experience lasted less than a month at initial characterization but remained present at the 72-day follow-up. Examination showed no sinonasal pathology.

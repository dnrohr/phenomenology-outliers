# Participant 6: total relocation to a dark hallway

## Account

During guided group meditation he stopped hearing the guide and stopped being physically present in the room; all of him seemed located in a dark hallway, as real as ordinary surroundings. Fear and discomfort left him afraid to sleep for half a month and he stopped meditating. His tentative explanation was unusually deep relaxation.

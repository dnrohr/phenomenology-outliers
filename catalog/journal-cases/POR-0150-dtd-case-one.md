# Known routes work until the slightest detour

## Account

Pt1 learned a few simple routes and recognized landmarks, but a small deviation left her disoriented. Repetition could eventually support performance in a virtual environment; the persistent weakness was acquiring a flexible cognitive map rather than seeing landmarks or following a drilled sequence.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

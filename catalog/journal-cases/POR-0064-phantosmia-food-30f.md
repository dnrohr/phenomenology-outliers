# COVID phantosmia patient 1: recurring phantom food odor

## Account

After confirmed COVID-19, this 30-year-old woman developed a food-like odor without an external source. It occurred about four times per week and lasted between one and three months. She had not reported anosmia or hyposmia during the acute illness and had no sinonasal pathology on examination. The phantom odor did not persist at the recorded follow-up.

# 72-year-old woman: a hand moving by itself, later restrained by inner command

## Account

After a frontal and callosal infarct, her right hand grasped and manipulated objects without intention. Initially she noticed only when a therapist pointed it out. Later she felt the hand moving by itself, held it down with the left hand, and eventually restrained it by internally telling it not to move. Ownership remained intact while authorship and control were disrupted, then partly rebuilt through inner speech.

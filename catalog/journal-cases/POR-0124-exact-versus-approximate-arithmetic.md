# Exact arithmetic lost while approximate quantity remains

## Account

After a large left-hemisphere infarct, the patient had severe global aphasia and could not reliably produce exact answers. She nevertheless compared magnitudes, matched numerals across formats, retained everyday numerical knowledge, and selected plausible approximate answers. Exact verbal calculation and approximate quantity therefore came apart within one person.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

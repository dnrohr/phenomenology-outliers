# Touch remains present but floats free of a body map

## Account

E.C. had pronounced difficulty on tasks requiring visuospatial representation of body parts while semantic and linguistic knowledge and representations of objects and animals were normal. Body mapping failed without a general spatial or naming disorder.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Relations and rotations remain clear without object pictures

## Account

The patient struggled to generate the color and surface appearance of objects yet could inspect layouts and mentally transform spatial relations. Visual imagery divided into object-detail and spatial-coordinate formats.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

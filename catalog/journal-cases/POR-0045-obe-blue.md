# Participant 7: free movement through a world dyed blue

## Account

After meditation a nonphysical body felt numb and heavy. Tree, floor, walls, and surroundings looked dyed blue by moonlight. He felt watched by the tree but mainly curious; he could move up, down, left, and right, experiencing tangibility, tiredness, and freedom. The account combines global color transformation with voluntary navigation in an altered body-frame.

# 63-year-old woman: a persistent woman figure elaborated into ghosts

## Account

After severe visual impairment she progressed from black shadows to people and cars, then a persistent long-haired woman. Closing her eyes or praying briefly removed the figure. She later believed herself cursed; the woman followed her, brought ghosts, and appeared with a family portrait or rope. Terror, insomnia, screaming, and changing insight show how a perceptual anomaly can acquire agency and a threatening explanatory structure.

# Known people become strangers on the telephone

## Account

K.H. heard speech content normally but could not recognize familiar speakers by voice. Identity access from vocal timbre failed while language comprehension and face recognition were available.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

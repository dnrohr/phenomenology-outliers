# Faces, ears, and hair stretch after an infection

## Account

Soon after a respiratory illness, the child began seeing faces, ears, and hair elongated horizontally and vertically. Eye, brain, developmental, and psychological examinations were otherwise unremarkable, and consistent drawings externalized the experienced geometry.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

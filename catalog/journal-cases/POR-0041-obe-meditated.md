# Participant 3: deliberate distancing of consciousness from the body

## Account

Through meditation she felt consciousness and body separate far enough that opening her eyes seemed likely to reveal her body. Fear prompted return, which felt like being dragged downward or falling from a cliff. The event seemed as real as touching a table. She continued practicing, interpreted it spiritually, and sought a deeper repeat.

# Only faces become demonic and socially intolerable

## Account

For years the man sometimes saw his own reflection as demonic and also experienced distortions in another person's face. The alteration drove avoidance and rumination about how others saw him, while other psychotic symptoms were not reported.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

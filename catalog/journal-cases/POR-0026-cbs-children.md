# 80-year-old man: colorful children who seemed to caress him

## Account

He saw colorfully dressed boys apparently caressing his arms, girls with red nails, and children riding bicycles. Episodes lasted seconds to minutes, increased in frequency, and were involuntary and anxiety-provoking. He retained insight that they were unreal and could sometimes stop a scene by opening and closing his eyes. The reported caress is preserved as visually apparent touch; the source does not establish a tactile hallucination.

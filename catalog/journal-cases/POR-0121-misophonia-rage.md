# 35-year-old man: ordinary office sounds trigger rage and compelled attention

## Account

Chewing, gum, crunching, lip-smacking, tapping, and a shaking leg triggered immediate anger or rage and an exaggerated attentional lock. He wanted to confront the source and make it stop, though he never acted aggressively. Symptoms began at twelve, worsened gradually, and eventually forced medical leave from an office. The response crossed sound and sight and felt radically disproportionate despite insight.

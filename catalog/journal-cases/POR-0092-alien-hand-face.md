# 77-year-old woman: hand stroked her face and hair against her will

## Account

While watching television, her left hand flung across view and stroked her face and hair without her will. She was terrified and could not stop it with the right hand. Purposeful-looking movements continued for about thirty minutes before control returned; numbness and weakness followed. The brief event distinguishes an action's organized form from the person's felt authorship.

# Patient P1: her left hand belonged to her son

## Account

P1 spontaneously and repeatedly said her left hand belonged to her son, while accepting ownership of the rest of the left arm. The claim was firm and persisted across changes in hand position. This unusually local boundary—hand disowned, proximal limb retained—shows body ownership can fragment at a joint rather than disappear for an entire side.

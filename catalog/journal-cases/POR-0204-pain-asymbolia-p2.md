# Noxious intensity rises without ordinary aversion

## Account

Patient 2 discriminated stronger from weaker noxious stimulation yet did not react with proportionate unpleasantness. Sensory scaling and affective urgency diverged.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Remote dates take longer but remain calculable

## Account

G.C. answered weekday questions beyond the range of memorized perpetual calendars. More remote dates took longer and recruited regions also active during mental arithmetic, supporting a practiced calculation process even though the method was not available as a detailed verbal report.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Place identity survives loss of facing direction

## Account

The patient recognized familiar surroundings and landmarks after a right posteromedial stroke but could not derive the correct direction from them. Knowing where he was did not supply which way he was facing or which turn would lead home.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

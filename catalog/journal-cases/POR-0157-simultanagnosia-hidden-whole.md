# Only one fragment is seen, but the unseen whole changes the body

## Account

R.M. usually reported only a small sector of a complex picture and failed to see the whole scene. Nevertheless, emotionally meaningful unseen configurations altered psychophysiological responses, indicating recognition outside reportable scene awareness.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

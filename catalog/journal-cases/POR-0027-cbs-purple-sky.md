# 93-year-old man: purple sky, flying cars, and moving objects

## Account

Over four days he saw objects move, the sky turn purple, and cars fly. Images commonly appeared against a white wall. Despite glaucoma, cataracts, and reduced acuity, he scored 30/30 cognitively and recognized the events as unreal. Repetitive blinking could end them. They stopped after his glasses were restored and visual input improved, linking blank space and deprivation to vivid color and motion construction.

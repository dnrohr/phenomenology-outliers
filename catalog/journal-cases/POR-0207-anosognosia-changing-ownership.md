# A paralyzed limb alternates between mine and not mine

## Account

F.B. denied paralysis and sometimes disowned the affected limb, but ownership judgments shifted with task and cueing. Awareness of movement capacity and belonging was actively reconstructed rather than all-or-none.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

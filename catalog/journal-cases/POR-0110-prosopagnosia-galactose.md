# Woman with lifelong face blindness: faces, navigation, and imagery briefly sharpened

## Account

A woman with lifelong prosopagnosia unexpectedly reported broad temporary improvement after taking galactose: faces became recognizable, mental imagery more vivid, and navigation easier. The authors present this as a first case observation, not established treatment. Phenomenologically, the within-person contrast is striking because three normally weak constructions—identity from faces, inner pictures, and spatial orientation—became available together.

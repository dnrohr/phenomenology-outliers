# Participant 5: a nocturnal hospital visit apparently matched next day

## Account

After failing to reach an aunt's hospital room, she experienced visiting it during sleep: doctors admitted her, required handwashing, and led her through an outside hallway. Next day she felt the real route was identical and trembled. Having had such episodes since childhood, she considered them normal, vivid, real, inherited, and a privilege. The claimed match is recorded without adjudication.

# Learning looks normal until days later

## Account

Patient 1 learned stories and designs to ordinary short-delay levels, yet much more was lost over days or weeks. The subjective complaint concerned memories that seemed successfully stored and then disappeared outside standard test windows.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Only noses and mouths warp while identity remains clear

## Account

After a right temporo-occipital infarct, faces appeared as if through a convex lens: noses narrowed and stretched toward small round mouths. Familiar identity, objects, color, reading, writing, and complex-scene description remained intact.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

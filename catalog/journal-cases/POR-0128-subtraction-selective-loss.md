# Subtraction collapses while multiplication remains available

## Account

M.A.R., a left-handed patient with a right parietal lesion, showed the complementary pattern to B.O.O.: subtraction was grossly impaired while multiplication and addition were relatively spared. Arithmetic operations did not behave as a single faculty.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

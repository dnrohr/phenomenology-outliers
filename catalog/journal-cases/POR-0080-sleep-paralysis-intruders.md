# 30-year-old woman: escalating intruders during sleep paralysis

## Account

Across three episodes she awoke conscious but unable to move. First, women whispered around a hotel bed and seemed to approach. Next, an absent colleague's voice and bangles drew near in an office rest room. Finally, she saw a girl, felt a man lie beside and choke her, and experienced chest pressure while unable to scream. Each ended on full awakening; fear and sleep avoidance intensified.

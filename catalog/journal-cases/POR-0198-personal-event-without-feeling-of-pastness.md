# A remembered event loses its felt pastness

## Account

Around seizures, familiar material could arrive with an abnormal certainty of prior occurrence or remembered content could lose its ordinary temporal placement. Content and the metacognitive feeling that marks it as past were dissociable.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

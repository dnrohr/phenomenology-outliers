# Imagined space repeatedly collapses during construction

## Account

P04 described the central problem as getting the space itself: attempted settings felt compressed or unstable. The limitation was not simply low visual vividness but failure to maintain a coherent spatial field for an unfolding event.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

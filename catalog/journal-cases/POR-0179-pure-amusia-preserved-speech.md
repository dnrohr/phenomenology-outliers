# Music loses structure while speech and everyday sounds remain meaningful

## Account

After brain injury, a young nonprofessional musician showed impaired musical processing with normal speech and environmental-sound recognition. Auditory access was present, but organization specific to melody and related musical components was selectively disrupted.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# 15-year-old girl: numb but not exactly sad, unable to separate fear, anger, and sadness

## Account

She reported daily numbness, no happiness or interest, yet said she was not exactly sad. Trembling did not tell her whether she felt fear, sadness, or anger. Wanting connection but having nothing to say, she withdrew and felt absent from others' lives. She called herself a hollow tree, with headaches and chest tightness carrying distress that remained hard to identify verbally.

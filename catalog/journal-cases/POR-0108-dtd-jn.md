# J.N.: no mental map even for repeatedly traveled streets

## Account

J.N. could not visualize places in space or recognize a street driven many times. After leaving a cinema for the bathroom she could not find her seat; after exiting a building she might circle it to rediscover the entrance. She was verbally accomplished and read rapidly, with no face-recognition deficit. The lifelong problem was specific enough that others had mistaken it for inattention.

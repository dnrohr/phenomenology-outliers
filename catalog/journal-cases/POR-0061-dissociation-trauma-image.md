# P13: trauma memories remove the self from the physical body

## Account

When mental images of childhood events appeared, P13 felt removed from the physical body and found the state difficult to explain. Low mood could likewise produce the feeling of observing oneself. The account links internally generated autobiographical imagery, affect, and a displaced point of view, rather than describing detachment as a content-free or random state.

# P4: blanking out and losing the conversational thread

## Account

P4 sometimes realized they had completely blanked out and could not recover what they had been thinking or what another person had said. Familiar places could also feel unfamiliar. The account joins a gap in conscious thought and conversational memory to disturbed familiarity, without implying global amnesia. Awareness often arrived only after the missing interval.

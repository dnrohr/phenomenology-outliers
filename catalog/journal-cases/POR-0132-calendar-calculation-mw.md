# Calendar regularities used without an explicit algorithm

## Account

M.W. could exploit repeating calendar regularities and answer dates outside ordinary reference-calendar ranges. Like many calendar savants, he offered little introspective account of how the answer was produced, separating successful structured computation from reportable procedure.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# 45-year-old woman: a door banging at sleep onset

## Account

She heard a door bang at sleep onset two or three times monthly, almost always while prone. Restless legs and very low iron stores co-occurred; she declined laboratory sleep recording because she could not sleep in unfamiliar surroundings. Iron replacement and gabapentin improved both patterns, and the phantom door sound eventually remained in remission.

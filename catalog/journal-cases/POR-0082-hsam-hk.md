# HK: near-perfect dated autobiography without sight

## Account

Blind since infancy, HK could retrieve ordinary dated events with near-perfect accuracy from about age eleven. Memories simply entered his mind; he pictured being there again in a nonvisual sense and recovered weather, companions, food, sequence, and emotion. Sounds, smells, touch, taste, anniversaries, and news served as triggers. He compared himself to a walking computer but said he could let distracting memories go.

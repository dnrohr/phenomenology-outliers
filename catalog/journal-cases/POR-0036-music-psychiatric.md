# Case-series patient: music in the context of schizophrenia

## Account

The third patient in this series experienced music without an acoustic source in the context of schizophrenia. The authors deliberately compare her with a hearing-impaired older adult and a young hemorrhage patient, showing that a similar auditory form can occur in very different settings. The searchable report supplies limited verbatim phenomenology, so no unreported melody, location, or degree of insight is added.

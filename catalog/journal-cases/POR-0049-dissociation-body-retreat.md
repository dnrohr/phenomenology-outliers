# P24: watching through the eyes without physically feeling

## Account

P24 described a physical retreat from embodiment: watching through the eyes and hearing their own speech while no longer properly feeling bodily experience. At other times extreme anger, shock, or stress produced a viewpoint over the shoulder, as if observing a third-person video game. The camera-like self still registered ongoing action but was no longer centered in the normally felt body.

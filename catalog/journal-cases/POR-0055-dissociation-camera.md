# P3: feeling like a camera rather than a person

## Account

In crowded, overwhelming situations, other people receded into background and P3 became “a camera” rather than a person. They felt outside the body, floating, simultaneously feeling nothing and everything. The state was threatening and could trigger panic. The camera metaphor indicates preserved registration of a scene but loss of participation, personal presence, and ordinary embodied point of view.

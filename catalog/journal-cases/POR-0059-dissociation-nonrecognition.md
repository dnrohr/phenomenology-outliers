# P22: familiar scenes become puzzlingly unrecognized

## Account

P22 found it puzzling to look at something and fail to recognize it as familiar. They also described arriving somewhere and suddenly not remembering how they got there. The study presents these as dissociative experiences: momentary loss of environmental familiarity and a gap in the experienced continuity of travel, while later awareness of both anomalies remained.

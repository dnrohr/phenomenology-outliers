# J: parents, husband, and self remain hidden behind visible faces

## Account

J could not identify parents, husband, or sometimes herself from a face, and assumed for years that faces simply were not sufficient for recognition. She still read emotion, intention, attractiveness, and likability normally and recognized objects. Peripheral features helped more than inner facial features. A television interview finally revealed that other people identify one another directly by face.

# Participant 8: ascent from a street bench to second-floor height

## Account

Sitting silently on a street bench, he closed his eyes and felt his viewpoint rise over his neck to second-floor height. Fear that he was leaving and would not return ended it. He recalled a breeze before ascent but no wind once outside. The memory lacked dream qualities; later he allowed he might have been half asleep.

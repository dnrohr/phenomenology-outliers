# Disgust disappears while other emotions remain recognizable

## Account

After insular and basal-ganglia damage, B.W. had marked difficulty recognizing disgust in faces and sounds and reported reduced experience of disgust, while many other emotions remained accessible. One affective category could be selectively thinned.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

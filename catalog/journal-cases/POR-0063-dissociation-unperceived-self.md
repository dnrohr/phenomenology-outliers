# P21: wondering whether others can perceive an absent-feeling self

## Account

While deeply dissociated and scarcely aware of self, P21 began wondering whether other people were aware of or could perceive them. The reduced felt presence of self generalized into uncertainty about social visibility. The paper groups this with dissociation contributing to paranoia, while the report itself remains a question about how one's anomalous self-experience appears from other minds.

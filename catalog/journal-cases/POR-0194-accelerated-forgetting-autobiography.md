# Recent weeks dissolve despite intact immediate recall

## Account

Patient 2 could converse and remember over minutes but later found recent personal events disproportionately inaccessible. Retention time, not initial experience or immediate registration, defined the anomaly.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

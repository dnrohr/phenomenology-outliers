# P12: a physically sound left hand that cannot be made to work

## Account

P12 sometimes could not make the left hand work despite knowing nothing was physically wrong. They also described looking down on themselves in bed or seeing themselves from behind while walking. Raised voices could trigger an immediate TV-screen viewpoint. Distressing voices and low mood led to mental shutdown and feeling nothing. Motor control, self-location, and emotion could therefore detach in related but distinguishable ways.

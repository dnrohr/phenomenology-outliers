# 82-year-old man: visual denial with profound memory and spatial loss

## Account

He denied cortical blindness after bilateral posterior cerebral artery infarcts. The presentation also included profound anterograde amnesia, about fifty years of retrograde amnesia, severe topographical disorientation, and partial failure to name objects by touch or sound. The report documents simultaneous disruption of current sensory access, awareness of deficit, personal history, and spatial orientation.

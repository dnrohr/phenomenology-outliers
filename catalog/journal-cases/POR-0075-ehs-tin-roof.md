# 46-year-old man: something striking a tin roof inside his head

## Account

For more than ten years he heard “something hitting a tin roof” inside his head at the beginning of sleep, one or two times monthly. At first he looked around for an external source. Episodes clustered with supine sleeping, insomnia, irregular sleep, and poor sleep hygiene. Behavioral sleep treatment improved both insomnia and the internal impact sound, followed by three years of remission.

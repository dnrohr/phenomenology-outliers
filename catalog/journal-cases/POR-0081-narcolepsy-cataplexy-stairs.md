# 28-year-old woman: fully aware while emotion removed muscular control

## Account

Beginning at sixteen she had irresistible daytime sleep, sleep paralysis, and hypnagogic hallucinations; class notes shifted from coherent writing into scribbles as sleep took over. Strong emotion triggered collapses several times daily. After becoming upset atop stairs, she fell and watched each stair approach her face while remaining unable to move. Awareness and vision continued while motor control vanished.

# A limb disappears from the body map when unseen

## Account

Patient 2 sensed an invisible nearby double while the left hand became unusable when not watched. He could not mentally locate the unseen limb, linking felt external presence with loss of internal body-space representation.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# P25: not outside the body, but not inside it either

## Account

P25 resisted the usual binary description of being either inside or outside the body: the experience was “not necessarily outside,” yet did not feel like being inside. This concise report marks an indeterminate self-location rather than a classic elevated out-of-body perspective. It is useful evidence that bodily presence can weaken without relocating to a definite point in external space.

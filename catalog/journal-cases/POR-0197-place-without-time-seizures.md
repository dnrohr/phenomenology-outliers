# Location stays known while chronological position disappears

## Account

Patient 2 showed transient temporal disorientation with much better spatial orientation. The components of being situated in a world—where and when—could fail separately.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

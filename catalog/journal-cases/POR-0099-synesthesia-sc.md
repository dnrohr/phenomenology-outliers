# SC: words and sounds become tastes, smells, pressures, and mouth-objects

## Account

For SC, hearing speech, speaking, reading, and inner speech automatically evoke complex tastes and smells, felt on tongue, mouth, and throat. Some words also create pressure or an object-like presence in the mouth; musical instruments and environmental sounds can trigger tastes too. Pleasant and unpleasant pairings have persisted lifelong, and nearly every tested word produced some concurrent sensation. Actual semantics, sound, and bodily mouth-feel interact without voluntary choice.

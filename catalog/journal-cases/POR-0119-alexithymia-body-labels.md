# Woman learning that cold hands and tight muscles can mean nervousness

## Account

She habitually treated emotional needs as unworthy and described distress through physical symptoms rather than feeling words. Naming emotions was so difficult that she repeatedly returned to worries and bodily complaints. With guidance she connected “nervousness” to cold extremities, tight scalp, rapid breathing, muscle tension, and trembling voice and movement. Therapy built a vocabulary linking interoceptive patterns to affective categories.

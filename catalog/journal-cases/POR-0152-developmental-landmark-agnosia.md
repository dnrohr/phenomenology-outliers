# Familiar places look visible but not familiar

## Account

Six-year-old L.G. failed to recognize familiar places and sometimes misidentified unfamiliar ones, while ordinary object recognition remained intact. Co-occurring congenital prosopagnosia linked two kinds of identity recognition without making either a general visual deficit.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

# Everyday sounds remain clear while speech becomes noise

## Account

Spoken conversation increasingly sounded like noise or buzzing, especially in crowds, while the patient could read and understand written sentences and communicate in writing. Hearing was not silence; speech lacked usable phonological structure.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

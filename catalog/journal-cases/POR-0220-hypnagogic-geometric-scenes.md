# Sour taste is absent on an otherwise normal taste palette

## Account

The second participant in the case series was also unresponsive to sour stimuli while bitter, sweet, and salty responses remained normal. A replicated within-modality dissociation shows that losing one taste quality need not flatten taste as a whole.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

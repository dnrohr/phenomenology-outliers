# A blind half-field without representational neglect

## Account

P3 lost the right visual field after surgery but retained splenial connections and did not develop spatial neglect. This within-series contrast separates absence of visual input from the attentional omission of a side of space.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

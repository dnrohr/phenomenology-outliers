# Internal air hunger restores fear absent from ordinary threats

## Account

Twin 2 showed the same counterintuitive contrast: profound fear during carbon-dioxide challenge despite diminished fear in ordinary life. The trigger source mattered more than a unitary capacity for fear.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

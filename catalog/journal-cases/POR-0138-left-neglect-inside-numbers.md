# The left side disappears inside two-digit numbers

## Account

After cardiac arrest, a man made distinctive errors on the left side of two-digit numerals. Further testing showed left neglect for words, drawings, and extrapersonal space despite alertness and substantial independence. Spatial attention operated within symbolic number strings as well as physical scenes.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

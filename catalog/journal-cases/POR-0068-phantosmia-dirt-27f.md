# COVID phantosmia patient 5: persistent dirt odor without prior smell loss

## Account

A 27-year-old woman who had not reported anosmia or hyposmia during acute COVID-19 later smelled dirt without a source roughly three times per week. The experience lasted one to three months and was still present at 69-day follow-up. No sinonasal pathology was observed, separating the qualitative odor from an obvious local obstruction.

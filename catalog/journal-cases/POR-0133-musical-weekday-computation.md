# Weekdays are computed through seven musical notes

## Account

F.C. mapped Monday through Sunday onto C through B and described thinking with the note names while calculating weekdays. He also had absolute pitch. Calendar computation was consciously scaffolded by a stable cross-domain mapping between a seven-day cycle and a seven-note sequence.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

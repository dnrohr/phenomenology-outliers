# Accurate adult arithmetic assembled slowly on the fingers

## Account

R.M. answered nearly all single-digit problems correctly but usually did not retrieve arithmetic facts directly. She counted up or down on her fingers, and for multiplication often retrieved a five-times-table result and then counted the remaining groups. High accuracy concealed a strikingly procedural, embodied route to elementary arithmetic.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

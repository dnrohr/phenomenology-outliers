# Real-world events return as fragments without their original order

## Account

After a structured campus walk, this patient recalled fewer details than immediate controls. Surviving details still referred to particular places and events, but their recall order no longer tracked the order in which the walk unfolded.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

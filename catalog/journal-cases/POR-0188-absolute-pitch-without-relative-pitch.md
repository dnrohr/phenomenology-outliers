# Notes have names while melodic intervals remain effortful

## Account

The case rapidly named isolated pitches and experienced note categories as stable labels, yet relative interval operations did not share the same automaticity. Absolute identity and relational musical structure used distinguishable formats.

## Why this case matters

This report constrains the catalog's taxonomy by separating capacities that ordinary experience tends to bundle together.

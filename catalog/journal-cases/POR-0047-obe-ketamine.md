# Participant 9: watching his own body walk ahead

## Account

After ketamine he became dizzy, walked, and saw his head and body moving ahead of his observing viewpoint for about five minutes. Walking still felt intentional, not automatic, despite the divided perspective. He felt confusion and euphoria rather than fear, judged it real, and interpreted it chiefly as drug-altered perception.

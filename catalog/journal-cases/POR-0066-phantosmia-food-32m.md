# COVID phantosmia patient 3: phantom food odor after smell loss

## Account

A 32-year-old man developed phantom food smells after an acute period that included anosmia and hyposmia. The odor appeared about twice per week for less than a month. No sinonasal abnormality was found, and the experience was no longer present at 58-day follow-up. The record captures a positive odor percept emerging after quantitative smell loss.

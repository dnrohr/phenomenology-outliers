# No waking pictures, detailed inner sound, and lifelike color dreams

## Account

The poster reports being unable to voluntarily picture anything while awake. In contrast, they can memorize and internally replay detailed sounds and voices with ease. During sleep they experience vivid, lifelike dreams in full detail and color.

The striking claim is not simply low imagery. It is a three-way split: waking visual generation is absent; waking auditory replay is strong; involuntary dream vision is vivid.

## Circumstances and course

The post presents the pattern as an enduring trait rather than a recent loss. The author wishes the visual capacity available in dreams could be “unlocked” while conscious. No medical history or formal assessment is provided.

Other people in the same thread describe related but nonidentical patterns: some retain fragments of dream imagery briefly after waking; some remember only that a movie-like dream occurred; one reports full aphantasia across waking senses but dreams with waking-life clarity. Those comments are corroborating accounts, not part of this subject's phenotype.

## Structure of the experience

- **Waking voluntary vision:** no reportable image.
- **Waking auditory imagery:** detailed sounds can be stored and replayed; voices are especially well retained.
- **Dream vision:** full-detail, colored, lifelike scenes.
- **Control:** auditory replay is voluntary; dream imagery is state-bound and cannot be summoned after waking.

## Key contrasts

`auditory imagery present` / `visual imagery absent`  
`dream vision vivid` / `waking visual generation absent`

## Why this case matters

It argues against treating imagery as either a single amodal faculty or a single visual gain setting. Modality and state both matter: voluntary generation, involuntary dreaming, and auditory replay vary independently within one account.

# Mental addition by fitting pictured digits together

## Account

The commenter describes mental arithmetic as a spatial manipulation of visible numeral shapes. For seven plus five, the rounded lower portion of the pictured 5 nests on top of the 7 to complete ten, while the upper “flag” remains as two more, yielding twelve. They compare the process to Tetris pieces moving into place.

The same commenter notices rhyme-driven substitution errors in writing: hearing one item internally can lead to writing a rhyming number or word, especially while typing.

## Structure of the experience

- **Representational object:** the visual shape and decomposable parts of Arabic numerals.
- **Operation:** spatial nesting and part reassignment instantiate carrying/decomposition.
- **Result:** the arithmetic relation is experienced through a transformation rather than only verbal calculation.
- **Cross-talk:** phonological similarity can intrude on written output.

## Key contrasts

`numerical magnitude` / `visual numeral anatomy`  
`intended written item` / `phonologically associated item`

## Why this case matters

It is a compact example of a task strategy that would be invisible in accuracy scores. Correct arithmetic can arise through idiosyncratic visuospatial operations on learned symbols.

## Source boundaries

The source provides one worked example, not evidence that all arithmetic uses this strategy.

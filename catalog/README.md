## Coverage snapshot

The first tranche is intentionally broad rather than prevalence-weighted:

- **Source classes:** 10 spontaneous reports, 10 journal cases, 3 long-form clinical/book cases.
- **Survey baseline represented:** visual and auditory imagery, faces, inner speech, reading, spatial cognition, time, autobiographical memory, conceptual thought, agency, memory search, metacognition, interoception, and bodily awareness.
- **New dimensions forced by accounts:** dream/waking dissociation, hypnagogia, orthographic concurrents, motion-only vision, pain unpleasantness, specific-emotion loss, cross-state color construction, and proprioceptive substitution.

Absence from this index means “not yet cataloged,” never “not a real form of experience.”
